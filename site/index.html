How the AI "sleeps"

Four steps, repeated every round. Qwen3-4B (full precision) runs on an Apple M2 with 16 GB.

01
WakeIt chats and collects facts. Only checked, approved facts are kept.
02
SleepIt studies them by training a tiny add-on (LoRA). The main model is never touched.
03
Exam220 questions: new facts, real knowledge and reasoning.
04
GateStrict rules decide if the new version may replace the old one.
